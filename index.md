Hello! I'm **Yanbaihui (Evelyn) Liu**, a Ph.D. student in the Robotics program at **Duke University**, advised by [Dr. Boyuan Chen](http://boyuanchen.com/).

My research lies at the intersection of **multimodal perception** and **robotics**. I’m particularly interested in developing systems that intelligently fuse information from diverse sensory inputs—such as vision, audio, and tactile data—to enable robust and adaptive behavior in the physical world.

Prior to Duke, I earned my **M.S. in EECS** from the **University of Michigan**, where I worked on visual odometry and sensor fusion in robotics. I completed my **B.S. in ECE** at the **Georgia Institute of Technology**, where I gained foundational experience in embedded systems and signal processing.

If you're interested in my work or want to collaborate, feel free to reach out at **yanbaihui.liu@duke.edu** — I'd love to chat!
